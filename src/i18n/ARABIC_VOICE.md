# Systemly Arabic product voice

Systemly uses natural professional Arabic suitable for Egyptian business software.

## Principles

- Translate the intended meaning, not the English sentence structure.
- Prefer short, direct instructions that sound natural inside an application.
- Keep the tone professional without using unnecessarily formal wording.
- Keep business names, customer names, service names, email addresses, URLs, IDs, and phone numbers unchanged.
- Use Latin digits with Arabic labels: `1,250 ج.م.` and `10:30 ص`.

## Approved terminology

| English | Arabic |
| --- | --- |
| Operations | إدارة العمل |
| Business | النشاط |
| Staff | فريق العمل |
| Appointment | موعد |
| Customer | عميل |
| Dashboard | لوحة التحكم |
| Workspace | نظام الإدارة |
| No-show | عدم حضور |
| Collect payment | تحصيل الدفع |

## Example

`The system behind your business.` is translated by meaning as:

`كل ما تحتاجه لإدارة نشاطك في مكان واحد.`

It must not be translated literally as `النظام الذي يدير نشاطك من الخلف`.
